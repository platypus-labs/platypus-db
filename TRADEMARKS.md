- **Refer to the project by its name**: write about PlatypusDB, review it, compare it, teach it,

- **Redistribute the official builds unmodified**, under their own name and icon: mirror the

- **Link to the project** with its name, and use the logo unmodified to link to the repository

- **Build and run your own changes** for yourself or inside your organization, under any name.

  approved or supported by the PlatypusDB project when it is not.
- **Using the name or the logo in other products, services or domain names**, or a name or logo
  similar enough to be confused with them.
- **Changing the logo** (colors, shape, mascot) or combining it with other marks, except to
  resize it.
- **Merchandise** with the name or the logo.

When in doubt, ask: open an issue on
[the repository](https://github.com/platypus-labs/platypus-db/issues) or contact the
